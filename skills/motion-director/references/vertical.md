# Social shorts (9:16)

Made to be watched muted on a phone, in a feed, by someone about to scroll away.

- **The frame:** 1080×1920 (`data-width="1080" data-height="1920"` on the root). Keep text inside the safe area:
  140 px from the top, 420 px from the bottom (the platform's caption and buttons), 64 px from the sides.
- **The first second decides.** Frame one already shows the point or a question in large type, fully readable,
  with a hit in the first 0.3 s; no logo first, no blank screen while something types in.
- **Captions are the film.** With a script, every word appears on screen in sync with the beat (word by word or
  phrase by phrase), large (70–110 px), high contrast, never more than two lines at once. The key word of each
  line gets the accent.
- **Cut fast, hold the payoff.** A change every 1–2 s; the line that matters holds for a full read.
- **Sound for both:** it must read muted, and reward sound: hits on each caption landing, the drop on the payoff.
- **End with the ask** on screen (follow, the link, the command) for at least 1.5 s.
- **Style:** kinetic type, or data story when the payoff is a number. A trial short is the hook and its payoff in
  10–20 s; a full short with several beats is the Vertical Short skill's film.
