# Teasers and stings (10–20 s)

A teaser is the trailer of a product, a release or a brand: the viewer leaves knowing its name and the one thing
it does, and wants the rest.

- **One action, shown once.** Pick the single moment that makes the product make sense (a prompt typed and its
  result, a file dropped and what comes out, a toggle and the change). Rebuild that one screen from the product's
  own markup, colours and copy (or its live page: `mo harvest <url>`); never a generic mock-up. A whole tour is the
  full launch film.
- **Shape (15 s at ~110 BPM, 8 bars):** bar 1 the name or the problem, landing on beat 1 · bars 2–5 the one action
  and its result, the camera pushing to where it happens · bars 6–7 the line (what it is, in under eight words) ·
  bar 8 name and link, held ~2 s. **At 20 s (120 BPM, 10 bars):** bar 1 the name or the problem · bars 2–6 the
  action and its result, with room for a second beat of the same action (the result used, or a before → after) ·
  bars 7–8 the line, or the one number that proves it · bars 9–10 name and link, held ~2.5 s.
- **For a release:** the one change that matters, before → after. **For a sting (6–10 s):** the mark built or
  revealed on the beat, then held.
- **Style:** kinetic type carries the name and the line; the product's own interface carries the action. Data story
  when the result is a number.
