# Changelog — Tally (sample project)

Sample material for trying the skill. Tally is a made-up invoicing app.

## 2.0.0 — 2026-10-01

### New
- **Recurring invoices.** Set an invoice to repeat weekly, monthly or yearly; Tally sends it on the day.
- **Pay by link.** Every invoice gets a payment link. Clients pay by card without an account.
- **Dashboard.** One screen with what's owed, what's late and what came in this month.

### Improved
- Invoices open 3x faster (median 1.2 s → 0.4 s).
- Late reminders go out automatically after 3, 7 and 14 days.

### Numbers from the beta
- 1,200 teams in the beta.
- Average time to get paid fell from 18 days to 9 days.
